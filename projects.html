Forced Focus App: C++ & QML

I feel a majority of apps are designed to keep you hooked for long periods of time. Because of that, I figured a lot of people may struggle to maintain focus while they're working. That was the main inspiration behind this app. The idea is the user will commit to not using an app for a certain period of time. If they ever attempt to go to the app, they will be forced to play a small game of tic-tac-toe, and then go back to what they were doing before.
